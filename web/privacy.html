Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you submit a prediction, we store your chosen display name and your predicted scores. This data is tied to a unique token that identifies your account across sessions.

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage — functionally similar to cookies — to persist the following data on your device:

  • Account token — keeps you identified so we can tie your future and past predictions together.
  • Theme preference — remembers whether you have chosen dark, light, or system mode.

No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used by this site.

Data retention

Your name and predictions are stored on our servers for the duration of the event. Local storage data persists in your browser until you clear it through your browser settings.

Your rights

You can clear all locally stored data at any time via your browser settings or using the button below. To request deletion of server-side data associated with your name, contact us.
